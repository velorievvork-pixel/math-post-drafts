# Evening post — 2026-10-12

**Topic:** The Goldbach Conjecture. In a 1742 letter to Leonhard Euler,
Christian Goldbach conjectured that every even integer greater than 2 can
be written as the sum of two primes (e.g. 4=2+2, 100=3+97=11+89=17+83=...).
It remains unproven nearly 300 years later, despite being one of the
oldest and most famous open problems in number theory. It has been
verified by computer for every even number up to 4×10¹⁸ (Tomás Oliveira e
Silva et al.), with some unreviewed 2025 preprints claiming extensions a
bit further, but a general proof is still missing.
