# Evening post — 2026-09-10

**Topic:** The Kakeya needle problem (1917) — the minimum area needed to
continuously rotate a unit line segment through every direction in the
plane. Kakeya's deltoid solution achieves area π/8 (verified numerically:
tangent chords of the deltoid have constant length 1, enclosed area = π/8).
Besicovitch (1928) later showed the true infimum is 0.

**News hook:** Hong Wang (NYU) and Joshua Zahl resolved the 3D Kakeya set
conjecture; Wang won the 2026 Fields Medal for it, only the third woman
ever to win the prize.
