# Morning post — 2026-11-28

**Topic:** The Euler line. In 1765 Leonhard Euler proved that for any
triangle, three famously different centers — the orthocenter (H, where
the altitudes meet), the centroid (G, the balance point), and the
circumcenter (O, center of the circle through the vertices) — always
lie on a single straight line. The centroid sits exactly 1/3 of the
way from O to H (OG : GH = 1 : 2). The nine-point circle's center also
lies on this line, at the midpoint of O and H.
