# Morning post — 2026-09-11

**Topic:** The "Go First Dice" — five identical 60-sided dice, numbered
1–300 with no number repeated across the set, that let any group of 2–5
players determine turn order with a single roll and zero possibility of a
tie. Designed by Eric Harshbarger (Auburn Univ. game/puzzle designer) with
Canadian researcher Paul Meyer, who found the winning 60-sided arrangement
via a large computer search around 2023, capping a ~15-year quest that
began with board game designer James Ernest's original question. Earlier
milestones: a 3-die (d6) solution for 3 players, a 4-die (d12) solution for
4 players, and an interim (bulkier) 5×d120 solution before the elegant
5×d60 set.

**Visualization angle:** contrasted the probability of at least one tie
when 2–5 players roll ordinary six-sided dice (17%, 44%, 72%, 91% —
computed directly via the standard "birthday-problem" combinatorics) against
the Go First Dice's guaranteed 0% tie rate.
