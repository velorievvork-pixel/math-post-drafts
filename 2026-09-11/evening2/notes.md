**Topic:** The Ulam spiral. In 1963, Stanislaw Ulam was doodling during a
boring lecture: he wrote the integers in a square spiral starting from 1 at
the center, then circled the prime numbers. To his surprise, the primes
clustered along diagonal lines far more than chance would predict — a
pattern that shows up no matter what number the spiral starts from. It was
striking enough to put the Ulam spiral on the cover of Scientific American
in March 1964.
