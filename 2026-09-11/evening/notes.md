# Evening post — 2026-09-11

**Topic:** The Hardy-Ramanujan "taxicab" number, 1729. In 1918, G.H. Hardy
visited Srinivasa Ramanujan in hospital (Putney, near London; Ramanujan was
ill with tuberculosis) and remarked that his taxi's number, 1729, seemed a
"rather dull" number. Ramanujan immediately replied that it was in fact
very interesting: it is the smallest positive integer expressible as the
sum of two cubes in two different ways — 1³+12³ = 1+1728 = 1729, and
9³+10³ = 729+1000 = 1729. It is now known as the Hardy-Ramanujan number
(also Ta(2), the second "taxicab number").
