# Evening post — 2026-10-10

**Topic:** The 2026 Fields Medals, announced July 23, 2026 at the ICM in
Philadelphia. Four medalists: Yu Deng (U. Chicago), John Pardon (Stony
Brook), Jacob Tsimerman (U. Toronto), and Hong Wang (NYU Courant / IHES).
Wang's win is the headline hook: in the medal's 90-year history (since
1936), she is only the third woman ever to receive it, after Maryam
Mirzakhani (2014) and Maryna Viazovska (2022) — no woman won in the
prize's first ~78 years. She is also the first Chinese woman to win it.
Wang was cited for her work in harmonic analysis / geometric measure
theory, including her proof (with Joshua Zahl) of the three-dimensional
Kakeya conjecture.
