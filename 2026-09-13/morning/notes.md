# Morning post — 2026-09-13

**Topic:** The Voronoi diagram. Scatter points on a plane, then color every
other location by which point it's closest to — the result splits space
into polygonal "territories" with no gaps and no overlaps. Formalized by
Georgy Voronoi in 1908 (building on Dirichlet's 1850 work), it shows up
everywhere in nature: giraffe skin patches, dragonfly wings, soap-film
boundaries, and biological cell packing all approximate the same
nearest-neighbor partition.
