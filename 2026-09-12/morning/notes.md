# Morning post — 2026-09-12

**Topic:** The Chaos Game. Pick a starting point inside a triangle. Repeatedly
choose one of the triangle's 3 corners at random and jump exactly halfway
toward it, plotting each new point. Despite every step being pure chance,
the accumulated points converge onto the Sierpinski triangle — a perfect
self-similar fractal. This "random iteration algorithm" is a classic
illustration of order emerging from chaos.

**Format:** 3-slide carousel (recommended) — slide1_hook.png (hook: the
rule stated plainly, only ~400 dots so no structure is visible yet),
slide2_fractal.png (reveal: 60,000 points, the fractal is unmistakable),
slide3_named.png (payoff: names it "The Sierpinski Triangle"). Also kept:
chaos_game.png (single combined before/after static image) and
chaos_game.gif (animated build-up, ~5s, 10fps, 600x338, captioned) as
