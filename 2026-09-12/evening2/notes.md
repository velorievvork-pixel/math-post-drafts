**Topic:** The Mobius strip. Take a strip of paper, give it a single
half-twist, and tape the ends together. It has only ONE side and ONE edge —
trace a finger along the surface (or the boundary) and you cover the whole
thing without ever crossing an edge. Named after August Ferdinand Mobius
(1858). A classic extra fact (not used in the caption to keep it punchy):
cutting it lengthwise down the middle doesn't give two loops — it gives one
longer loop with two full twists.
